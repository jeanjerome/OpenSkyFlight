✈️ WHAT'S IN OPENSKYFLIGHT 2.0
• Fly anywhere on Earth: real elevation and satellite imagery streamed as you fly, no API key
• Physically based atmosphere: sky, aerial perspective, sunrise, sunset, blue hour, stars and moon
• Volumetric clouds you can fly through, with their shadows on the ground and on the aircraft
• Terrain shadows across the valleys, aircraft self-shadowing
• Dassault Rafale in Armée de l'Air livery: moving control surfaces, landing gear, afterburner with shock diamonds, navigation and strobe lights, heat haze
• Six-degree-of-freedom flight model checked against the Rafale's published performance, fly-by-wire limits (+9 / −3.2 g)
• HDR pipeline: temporal anti-aliasing with upscaling (TAAU), auto exposure, bloom, AgX tone mapping
• Real airports and runways, takeoff and landing, HUD with approach guidance

🎬 ABOUT THIS VIDEO
In-engine footage, rendered by the simulator itself (Ultra settings) and captured frame by frame in 4K at 60 fps, with scripted flight paths and cameras. No video game engine, no pre-rendered scenery: just the browser.

🛠️ BUILT WITH
JavaScript · Three.js (WebGPU renderer, TSL shaders) · three-tile (terrain LOD) · takram three-geospatial (atmosphere, clouds)

🗺️ DATA & CREDITS
